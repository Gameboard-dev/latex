# FNRATE
**Long form:** Signal Connection Classification Error Rate

The share of signals accepted.

# Known Good Connection
^known-good

A connection made in the intended manner.

# W1
**Long form:** Wasserstein-1 Distance
**Printed as:** $W_1$

A distance between two distributions.

# Domain Shift
^shift

A change between the training and test data.
